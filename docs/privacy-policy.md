# Mini Sudoku — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-09-20

## What the app stores

Mini Sudoku keeps your game progress, statistics and language preference on
your device only. There is no account, and no server operated by the developer
receives gameplay data.

## Crash reports and usage analytics

If the app crashes, Firebase Crashlytics receives a report with the device
model, operating system version, app version and the error stack trace, so the
bug can be fixed. Firebase Analytics receives anonymous usage events (for
example, "game completed" with the difficulty and elapsed time). Neither
service receives your name, e-mail or contact details. Firebase is operated by
Google under its own privacy policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

## Advertising

Ads are served by Unity Ads. Unity and its partners may process the device's
advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location and ad interactions
to serve, measure, secure and — if you agreed — personalize ads. The choice
between personalized and non-personalized ads is asked when the app first
starts and can be changed in Settings. Unity's privacy policy:
https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

## Purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play. The app receives a purchase token and
sends it to our server, which verifies it with Google and credits the
purchase. We never receive payment details.

## Anonymous account

To keep the hint balance in step across purchases and rewarded ads, the app
creates an anonymous Firebase account: a random identifier with no name,
e-mail or phone number. Our server stores that identifier, the hint balance
and a ledger of credits and debits.

## Deleting your data

In the app: Settings → Delete my data. This erases the anonymous account and
its data on our server, the Firebase user, and everything stored on the
device. Without the app, or if it no longer opens: see
[delete-account.md](delete-account.md).

## Children

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children
under 13.

## Your choices

You can delete all local data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling
it.

## Changes

This policy changes when the app's features or SDKs change. The effective date
above is updated with every material revision; the history is visible in the
repository.

## Contact

Use the developer contact shown on the app's store listing.
