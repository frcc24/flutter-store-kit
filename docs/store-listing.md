# Store listing — Mini Sudoku

Paste-ready for Play Console → Grow → Store presence → Main store listing.
Set **en-US as the default language** first, then add pt-BR and es-419 under
Manage translations. Limits: title 30, short description 80, full
description 4000 characters.

## en-US (default)

**App name** (≤ 30)
```
Mini Sudoku
```

**Short description** (≤ 80)
```
Clean 9×9 sudoku. Three levels, one free hint per game, no clutter.
```

**Full description** (≤ 4000)
```
Mini Sudoku is the sudoku you play in a minute between two things: a clean
9×9 board, three difficulties, and nothing in the way.

WHAT YOU GET
• Puzzles with one unique solution, generated on your phone — no downloads.
• Easy, Medium and Hard. Mistakes are marked; you decide when to check.
• One free hint per game. Out of hints? Watch a short ad or buy a pack.
• Your game is saved the moment you leave and resumes where you stopped.
• Statistics: games completed and your best time per level.
• English, Portuguese and Spanish.

WHAT WE DO NOT DO
• No sign-up, no e-mail, no password.
• No timers that nag, no lives, no energy.
• Ads can be removed with a single purchase. Rewarded ads stay optional.

Built with Flutter and open source: github.com/frcc24/flutter-store-kit
```

## Keywords (where they live)

Play has no keyword field. Rank comes from the title, the short description
and the first lines of the full description, plus ratings and retention. The
words that matter here: sudoku, puzzle, 9×9, hint, offline, no account.
